# Stellons v0.4.14

## Release Name

Terraform Architecture Unification

---

## Objective

Eliminate duplicate Terraform artifact models.

---

## Architecture Before

InfrastructureIntent
↓
InfrastructureRequest
↓
TerraformGenerator
↓
GeneratorResponse

Validation
↓
TerraformProject

Packaging
↓
TerraformProject

Two competing artifact models.

---

## Problems Encountered

GeneratorResponse represented Terraform.

TerraformProject also represented Terraform.

This created unnecessary complexity.

---

## Resolution

Removed GeneratorResponse from the Terraform workflow.

Standardized on:

TerraformProject

---

## Architecture After

Prompt
↓
Intent
↓
TerraformProject
↓
Validation
↓
Packaging
↓
Export

---

## Technical Changes

TerraformGenerator returns TerraformProject.

GoalToTerraformService returns TerraformProject.

Validation consumes TerraformProject.

Packaging consumes TerraformProject.

---

## Lessons Learned

Every pipeline should have a single artifact representation.

---

## Impact

Reduced technical debt.

Prepared Stellons for governance and policy evaluation.
